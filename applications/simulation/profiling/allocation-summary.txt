FrameEffect object/control allocation accounts for 78.5% of these invocations.

together account for 99.8%. This count excludes allocator activity internal to
the C library/process startup. It comprises 100,262 `malloc`/`calloc` calls and
22,006 `realloc` growth calls; a `realloc` invocation may extend storage in
place rather than create a distinct new block.
